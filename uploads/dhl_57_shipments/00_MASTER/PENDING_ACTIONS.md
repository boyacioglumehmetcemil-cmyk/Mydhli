## What each shipment is waiting for

For every shipment above, the open items are identical:

1. Onward transport mode (ocean / road / air / multimodal) and preferred carrier
2. Confirmed delivery address and on-site receiving contact at the final destination
3. Required delivery date / cut-off
4. Confirmation that duties and taxes at the final destination will be settled by the consignee
5. Re-export or transit documents if the route crosses customs territories
6. Updated commercial invoice if the declared value has changed for re-export

Each shipment folder contains a **`09_Pending_Action_Note.docx`** that captures these items in
formal, customer-facing language ready to send to the consignee.
